What is WebUtility?

WebUtility is a free collection of online calculators built for everyday financial decisions. No accounts, no subscriptions, no data collection — just fast, accurate tools that work on any device.

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter is ever sent to a server or stored anywhere.

Why we built it

Most financial calculator sites are cluttered, slow, or require you to sign up just to do basic math. We built WebUtility because those calculations should be instant and free — always.

We keep the interface clean, the math accurate, and the tools focused on what actually matters.
