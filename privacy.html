Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

The short version: WebUtility does not collect, store, or sell any personal data. All calculations are never stored or logged. These happen instantly in your browser.

Who we are

WebUtility (webutility.pages.dev) is a free online calculator service. We provide financial tools including tip, profit & loss, budget, and mortgage calculators.

Our 'No Collection' Rules

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
  • We do not store any numbers or figures you enter into our calculators
  • We do not use cookies for tracking or advertising
  • We do not require account registration of any kind

All calculator logic runs entirely within your browser. Nothing you type is transmitted to our servers.

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand general site usage — such as how many people visit, which calculators are most used, and what devices are common. This data is aggregate and anonymous and cannot be used to identify individual users.

Third party services

WebUtility is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect basic request metadata (such as IP addresses) as part of their infrastructure and security services. This is governed by Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

We use Google Fonts to load typefaces. Google may log font requests. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

We may display advertisements served by Third Parties that may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Children's privacy

WebUtility is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by the updated date at the top of this page.
